A message to Ward 9

Leadership built for a changing city.

You deserve a candidate who is brilliant, vibrant, and strategic—someone who grounds every decision in reality, facts, and fiscal responsibility, while remaining agile enough to adapt to constantly evolving political and economic landscapes.

You deserve an artist and bridge-builder: a leader capable of connecting our seniors, youth, working parents, and folks from all walks of life with local business owners, non-profit organizations helping vulnerable community members, city staff, all three orders of government, and our shared natural environment.

Respect, honesty, dignity, energy, a growth mindset, humility, peace-making, humor, and hope are not just words—they are the foundational attributes I bring to serve as your elected Councillor for Ward 9.
