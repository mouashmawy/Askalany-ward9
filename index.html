Meet Mohamed

The bridge Ward 9 needs.

Mohamed Askalany brings institutional insight, diplomatic agility, and a results-oriented mindset to the work of representing Ward 9.

Effective local leadership takes more than raising an issue. It takes understanding municipal jurisdictions, identifying the right policy and funding avenues, and bringing residents, City staff, public-sector partners, community organizations, and local businesses around the same table.

Mohamed’s approach is proactive and practical: listen closely, navigate strategically, build a workable path forward, and keep residents informed from first conversation to final result.
