Welcome to Ward 9

The heart of Kitchener.

Ward 9 is the economic, cultural, and administrative heart of Kitchener. It deserves representation that understands where decisions are made and how to move them forward.

Connected leadership

Local concerns rarely stop at one government’s door.

Kitchener is part of the Regional Municipality of Waterloo. Residents pay taxes to both orders of municipal government, and each council sets its own budget and service responsibilities.

Effective Ward 9 advocacy starts by identifying whether an issue belongs with the City, the Region, or a partner—and then building the coalition, policy path, and funding strategy required to deliver.

Who handles what

Know the right door to knock on.

A practical overview based on the City of Kitchener’s official City and regional services guide.

City of Kitchener

Local municipal services

  • Local roads, traffic operations, parking, and winter maintenance
  • Parks, trails, arenas, community centres, and recreation
  • Fire services and many local bylaws
  • Planning, building, licensing, and economic development
  • Water distribution and sewage collection

Region of Waterloo

Regional services

  • Grand River Transit, ION, and regional roads
  • Police, paramedic services, and public health
  • Social services and regional housing
  • Garbage, recycling, green bin, and landfill services
  • Water treatment and wastewater management

Ward 9 focus

From casework to long-term planning.

Mohamed’s Ward 9 approach combines direct resident support with sustained work on infrastructure, budgets, business vitality, public safety, environmental stewardship, and reliable mobility.

The aim is to make City Hall approachable, report clearly on what can be done, and champion Ward 9 priorities with the decision-makers responsible for delivering them.
