Election Day

Monday, October 26, 2026

Ward 9 voting places will be open from 10 a.m. to 8 p.m. You may vote at any election-day voting place in Ward 9.

Bring identification that confirms your name and residence. Voting information can change, so confirm the latest details with the City of Kitchener before you go.

Advance voting

Vote early from October 13–17.

Advance voting is available at several locations across Kitchener, regardless of the ward you live in.

October 13–15

11 a.m. to 7 p.m. at six community and City locations.

October 16

11 a.m. to 7 p.m. at City Hall, Fairview Park Mall, and the Sunrise Shopping Centre drive-through.

October 17

11 a.m. to 7 p.m. at Fairview Park Mall and Sunrise Shopping Centre; Kitchener Market voting runs 9 a.m. to 2 p.m.

Who can vote

Municipal voter eligibility.

You are eligible to vote in Kitchener’s municipal and school board election if, on October 26, 2026, you:

  • are a Canadian citizen;
  • are at least 18 years old;
  • reside in Kitchener, or are the owner or tenant of land in Kitchener, or the spouse of that owner or tenant; and
  • are not prohibited from voting.
